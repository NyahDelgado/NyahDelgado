## 📌 Project Overview

**Free Coffee — Arrupe Hall Booth 7** is a promotional poster designed to advertise a free coffee event.

The design focuses on communicating the **event title, location, date, and promotional message** while creating a warm, inviting, and visually appealing experience.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this activity was to apply fundamental **presentation and graphic design principles** to create an effective visual communication piece.

The design was created to:

- 👀 Capture the viewer's attention
- 📖 Present information clearly
- 🎨 Create a cohesive visual identity
- ☕ Communicate the coffee theme
- 💼 Produce a polished and professional design

---

## 🎨 Design Principles Applied

### 🎯 1. Visual Hierarchy

The largest and most prominent element is **"FREE COFFEE"**, immediately directing the viewer's attention to the main message.

Supporting information is presented in smaller sizes, creating a clear reading order:

**Main Message → Location → Date → Illustration → Promotional Details**

This allows the audience to understand the purpose of the poster quickly.

---

### 🎨 2. Color Harmony

The design uses a warm, coffee-inspired color palette.

| Color | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Dark Green** | Adds contrast and visual identity |
| 🤎 **Brown** | Reinforces the coffee theme |
| 🟤 **Cream / Beige** | Creates warmth and comfort |
| ❤️ **Dark Red** | Adds subtle emphasis |

The combination creates a **cozy, friendly, and inviting atmosphere**.

---

### ⚖️ 3. Balance

The poster uses a balanced vertical composition.

The title occupies the upper section, while the coffee illustration and event information are distributed throughout the design. Coffee bean graphics around the edges help balance the composition.

**Result:** The design feels organized without appearing overcrowded.

---

### 🔤 4. Typography

Different font styles and sizes were used to establish visual hierarchy.

The bold display typeface makes **"FREE COFFEE"** immediately noticeable, while the script-style typography adds a relaxed and friendly personality.

**Result:** Typography makes the poster both expressive and informative.

---

### 🖼️ 5. Imagery

The illustration of a person drinking coffee reinforces the main concept of the poster.

Decorative coffee bean graphics are also used to strengthen the theme.

**Result:** The visual elements make the poster more engaging and help communicate the subject without relying entirely on text.

---

### ⚪ 6. White Space

Empty space is intentionally used between the major sections of the poster.

This prevents the design from becoming too crowded and allows the viewer to focus on important information.

**Result:** The poster remains clean, readable, and visually comfortable.

---

### 🔄 7. Consistency

The same visual language is maintained throughout the design using:

- 🎨 A consistent color palette
- 🔤 Complementary typography
- ☕ Coffee-related imagery
- 📐 Structured spacing
- 🖼️ Repeated visual elements

**Result:** The overall presentation feels unified and professional.

---

## 🛠️ Creative Process

### 💭 Step 1 — Concept Development

The concept was based on creating a **warm and inviting coffee advertisement** designed to attract students.

### 🎨 Step 2 — Color Selection

Coffee-inspired colors were selected to create a cozy atmosphere and visually connect the design to the subject.

### 📐 Step 3 — Layout Planning

The information was arranged according to importance, with the main message receiving the strongest visual emphasis.

### 🖼️ Step 4 — Adding Visual Elements

Coffee beans, illustrations, and decorative elements were added to reinforce the coffee theme and improve visual interest.

### 🔍 Step 5 — Final Refinement

The final design was reviewed for:

- ✅ Readability
- ✅ Contrast
- ✅ Balance
- ✅ Visual hierarchy
- ✅ Consistency
- ✅ Overall presentation

## 📊 Design Principle Summary

| Principle | How It Was Applied | Result |
|:---:|---|---|
| 🎯 **Hierarchy** | Large title and varied text sizes | Guides attention |
| 🎨 **Color** | Coffee-inspired palette | Creates visual identity |
| ⚖️ **Balance** | Distributed text and imagery | Creates stability |
| 🔤 **Typography** | Bold and script fonts | Adds personality |
| 🖼️ **Imagery** | Coffee illustrations | Supports the message |
| ⚪ **White Space** | Clear spacing between sections | Improves readability |
| 🔄 **Consistency** | Repeated styles and colors | Creates unity |

---

## ✨ Reflection

This activity demonstrated that effective presentation design is not simply about making something visually attractive. Each element should have a purpose and contribute to the overall message.

By applying **visual hierarchy, color harmony, balance, typography, imagery, white space, and consistency**, the poster became easier to understand and more engaging for the audience.

The final design communicates the coffee promotion quickly while creating a **warm, friendly, and inviting visual experience**.
