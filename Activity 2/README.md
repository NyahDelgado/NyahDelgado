## 📌 Project Overview

**NATURA — Live Simply** is a visual identity concept developed for this activity.

The purpose of this project was to explore how **color and typography can work together** to create a consistent, recognizable, and professional brand identity.

The visual direction is inspired by **nature, wellness, simplicity, and modern elegance**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective was to create a visual system that communicates:

- 🌿 Nature
- 💚 Wellness
- ✨ Elegance
- 🧘 Calmness
- 🧼 Simplicity
- 💼 Professionalism

The selected colors and typefaces were designed to work together across different branding materials.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette

The color palette is inspired by natural environments and uses muted, calming tones.

| Color | Hex Code | Meaning & Purpose |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| 🟢 **Forest Green** | `#1B5E3B` | Trust, growth, nature, sustainability |
| 🌿 **Sage Green** | `#A7C4A0` | Balance, harmony, wellness |
| 🤎 **Warm Beige** | `#EDED59` | Warmth, simplicity, comfort |
| ⚫ **Charcoal** | `#2E2E2E` | Clarity, focus, contrast |
| 🤍 **Soft White** | `#FAFAF8` | Cleanliness, minimalism, freshness |

### 🌲 Forest Green

The primary brand color represents **nature, growth, trust, and sustainability**. It gives the brand a grounded and natural feeling.

### 🌿 Sage Green

Sage green provides a softer complement to the primary green. It communicates **calmness, balance, and wellness**.

### 🤎 Warm Beige

Warm beige introduces warmth into the visual identity while maintaining a clean and minimal appearance.

### ⚫ Charcoal

Charcoal is used primarily for text and supporting elements. It provides strong contrast while being softer than pure black.

### 🤍 Soft White

Soft white provides breathing room and helps maintain the **clean and minimalist** character of the design.

---

## 🔤 Typography

The typography system combines an elegant serif typeface with a modern sans-serif typeface.

### ✨ Primary Typeface — Playfair Display

**Playfair Display** is used for the main brand name and prominent headings.

It communicates:

- ✨ Elegance
- 💎 Sophistication
- 🏛️ Classic quality
- 🌿 Timelessness

The serif design gives **NATURA** a distinctive and premium appearance.

---

### 🧼 Secondary Typeface — Montserrat

**Montserrat** is used for supporting information, labels, and secondary text.

It provides:

- ✅ Clean readability
- ✅ Modern appearance
- ✅ Simplicity
- ✅ Professionalism
- ✅ Strong visual clarity

Its clean geometric structure creates a strong contrast with Playfair Display.

---

## ⚖️ Typography Pairing

The two fonts were selected to create a clear visual hierarchy.

| Typeface | Role | Personality |
|---|---|---|
| **Playfair Display** | Brand name & headings | Elegant • Classic • Sophisticated |
| **Montserrat** | Body & supporting text | Clean • Modern • Readable |

### 💡 Design Relationship

```text
PLAYFAIR DISPLAY
        ↓
Elegant & expressive
        +
MONTSERRAT
        ↓
Clean & modern
        =
Balanced Brand Identity
