## 📌 Activity 3: Social Media Infographics & Mini Project Documentation

### 🎯 Project Overview

**Mental Health Matters** is a social media infographic created to raise awareness about mental health and encourage people to recognize stress, practice healthy habits, and seek support when needed.

The infographic presents important information in a **simple, friendly, and visually engaging format** that is suitable for social media.

---

## 💡 Concept

The main concept is to promote **mental health awareness, self-care, and support for others**.

The infographic focuses on:

- 🧠 Understanding mental health
- 💭 Knowing why mental health matters
- 📚 Identifying common stressors
- ⚠️ Recognizing signs of stress
- 🌱 Learning healthy ways to stay well
- 🤝 Encouraging people to seek support

The final message:

> 💚 **"Speak Up, Reach Out, Support One Another."**

was chosen to create a positive and supportive ending.

---

## 🎨 Design Choices

### 🌿 Color Palette

The infographic uses a calming and approachable color palette.

| Color | Purpose |
|:---:|---|
| 🟢 **Muted Green** | Represents calmness, balance, and well-being |
| 🔵 **Dark Teal** | Provides contrast and emphasizes headings |
| 🌸 **Soft Pink** | Represents care, warmth, and compassion |
| ⚪ **White** | Keeps the design clean and readable |

These colors work together to create a **calm, supportive, and positive visual experience**.

---

## 🔤 Typography

A bold and rounded display font was used for the major headings to make them stand out.

The body text uses a simpler style to maintain **readability and clarity**.

The difference in font size between headings and body text creates a strong **visual hierarchy**, allowing viewers to quickly identify the most important information.

---

## 🖼️ Visual Elements

Friendly illustrations were included to make the topic more approachable.

### Featured Visuals

- 🧠 **Smiling Brain** — represents mental well-being
- 😟 **Stressed Student** — represents academic and social pressure
- 🌱 **Peaceful Person** — represents positive mental health
- ✨ **Decorative Elements** — add visual interest and separate information

The illustrations help communicate emotions and ideas without relying entirely on text.

---

## 📐 Layout

The infographic uses a **vertical, section-based layout**.

Information is organized into clear sections:

| # | Section |
|---:|---|
| 01 | 🧠 Mental Health Matters |
| 02 | 💡 What It Is |
| 03 | ❤️ Why It Matters |
| 04 | 📚 Common Stressors |
| 05 | ⚠️ Signs You Might Be Stressed |
| 06 | 🌱 Tips to Stay Well |
| 07 | 🤝 Speak Up, Reach Out, Support One Another |

This structure creates a natural flow:

**Understanding → Awareness → Recognition → Action → Support**

---

## 🛠️ Creative Process

### 1. 💭 Planning

The topic of mental health was selected based on its importance to students and young people.

### 2. 📝 Organizing Information

The information was divided into short sections to prevent the infographic from becoming too text-heavy.

### 3. 🎨 Choosing the Visual Style

A calming color palette, rounded shapes, friendly illustrations, and bold typography were selected to match the topic.

### 4. 📐 Designing the Layout

The content was arranged vertically using organized sections, rounded containers, illustrations, and clear headings.

### 5. 🔍 Reviewing the Design

The final infographic was reviewed for:

- ✅ Readability
- ✅ Color consistency
- ✅ Visual balance
- ✅ Information hierarchy
- ✅ Overall presentation

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

This infographic aims to:

- 🧠 Raise awareness about mental health
- ⚠️ Help people recognize signs of stress
- 🌱 Encourage healthy coping habits
- 💬 Reduce the stigma surrounding mental health
- 🤝 Encourage people to seek support
- 💚 Promote a supportive community

---

## ✨ Design Reflection

Creating this infographic demonstrated how **visual design can be used as a powerful communication tool**.

Instead of presenting mental health information as large blocks of text, the combination of **color, typography, illustrations, and organized sections** makes the information easier to understand and more engaging.

The overall design aims to communicate feelings of:

> 🌿 **Calmness**  
> 💚 **Support**  
> 🌸 **Care**  
> ✨ **Positivity**

Most importantly, the infographic reminds viewers that **asking for help is okay and that no one has to face their struggles alone.**
